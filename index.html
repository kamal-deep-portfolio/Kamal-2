Behind the work

My tools have changed.
My responsibility
has grown.

I started in visual design. Learning UX helped me ask better questions. Learning to code let me stay accountable to the finished experience.

At Amazon, I’ve worked on products behind learning and talent-development programs within Customer Service. The work connects different responsibilities, from facilitators and program managers to leaders.

How I work now

I use Figma Make, Kiro, and Cline for AI-assisted exploration, rapid prototyping, and development. I bring the UX judgment to validate the output, check the details, and decide what deserves to ship.

Accessibility reviews, design guidance, and workshops are also part of how I contribute to a team.
